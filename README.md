# 🚀 Role-Based GPT Prompts

Turn AI into a personal mentor — not just a chatbot.

Role-Based GPT Prompts are pre-crafted, role-focused inputs that convert generative AI into expert advisors. Whether you're a student preparing for competitive exams, an engineer refining algorithm skills, or an HR professional designing learning paths and interviews — these prompts make AI act like a dedicated mentor.

## Why This Project
Many learners and teams struggle with:
- No personal mentorship or tailored guidance
- Confusion about what to study and when
- Generic AI answers that lack direction or follow-through

These role-based prompts give AI a consistent persona and a structured approach, producing actionable strategies, study plans, reviews, and coaching — just like a human mentor.

---

## Who This Helps
- Students preparing for exams like GATE
- Developers learning Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA)
- Hiring managers and HR specialists building training, interview, or performance coaching workflows
- Educators and mentors who want guided, repeatable AI interactions

---

## What’s Inside
Each prompt is crafted to act as a role-specific mentor with step-by-step guidance.

- ✅ **GATE Exam Advisor Prompt**
  - Emulates a GATE mentor with 20+ years of experience
  - Capabilities:
    - Analyze subject-wise weightage
    - Build prioritized study plans
    - Suggest efficient revision strategies
    - Identify low-ROI topics to skip or deprioritize

- ✅ **DSA Role-Based Advisor Prompt**
  - Acts as a full-time DSA mentor from beginner → advanced
  - Capabilities:
    - Structured learning path from fundamentals to advanced topics
    - Problem-solving practice plans and progress tracking
    - Reviews solutions, points out bugs, and suggests optimizations
    - Keeps learners accountable with regular checkpoints

## Quick Start
1. Open your preferred AI chat platform (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.).
2. Copy one of the prompts from this repository.
3. Paste it into the chat and start the conversation.
4. Interact naturally — ask for clarifications, schedules, mock interviews, or feedback.

---

## How to Use Prompts Effectively
- Provide context: share current level, goals, timelines, and constraints.
- Ask for a step-by-step plan and milestones (weekly/day-wise).
- Request checkpoints and measurable outcomes (e.g., "By week 4 I should solve medium-level arrays problems in 45 minutes").
- Save the conversation as a template to reuse and iterate.

---

## Examples & Use Cases for HR
These prompts are especially useful to HR professionals for:

- Onboarding & Training Plans
  - Generate a 30/60/90 day technical learning plan for a new hire
  - Create curated resource lists and exercises for role-specific skills

- Interview Design & Evaluation
  - Produce interview question sets tailored to experience levels
  - Create rubrics for technical and behavioral evaluations
  - Simulate interview scenarios and role-play candidate responses

- Performance Coaching & Career Growth
  - Generate personalized development plans
  - Recommend learning paths to close skill gaps
  - Create templates for regular feedback and follow-ups

- Learning Program Management
  - Design cohort schedules, assignments, and assessment rubrics
  - Estimate time requirements and ROI for different training modules

Sample prompt for HR (short):
> "Act as a Senior Engineering Manager. Create a 12-week onboarding and technical ramp-up plan for a mid-level backend engineer joining a fintech startup. Include milestones, learning resources, weekly hands-on tasks, and success metrics."

---

## Tips & Best Practices
- Be explicit about the role: e.g., "Act as a GATE mentor with 20 years experience" or "Act as a senior hiring manager."
- Set constraints: timelines, number of hours per week, tools allowed.
- Request deliverables: e.g., "Give a weekly checklist, sample problems, and a revision plan."
- Iterate: refine prompts by asking for more detail, summaries, or alternate strategies.
- Maintain a copy of the best prompt variations as templates for your team.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Q: Can I use these prompts with any chat AI?
A: Yes — they’re platform-agnostic. Minor rewording might help depending on the model.

Q: Are these prompts customizable?
A: Absolutely. Add your context (skill level, deadlines, constraints) to get tailored advice.

Q: Do these replace a human mentor?
A: They help replicate many aspects of mentorship (planning, feedback, practice). For nuanced human judgment or emotional coaching, combine prompts with human oversight.
